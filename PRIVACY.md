# Privacy Policy – Video Focus

Video Focus does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The extension runs only on youtube.com pages and only changes how the page is displayed (hides elements via CSS).
- The only thing it stores is one on/off setting (`focusOn`) in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It never leaves your device.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics, no tracking and no remote code.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/nikay99/video-focus-yt/issues
