Privacy Policy – Video Focus

Video Focus does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The extension runs only on youtube.com pages and only changes how the page is displayed (it hides elements using CSS).
  • It stores one on/off setting (focusOn) in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This setting never leaves your device.
  • It makes no network requests and uses no analytics, tracking or remote code.

Video Focus is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
