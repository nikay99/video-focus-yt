# Chrome Web Store listing (draft, English)

**Name:** Video Focus
**Summary (max 132 chars):** Hides recommendations, comments and the header on YouTube video pages. Black background, only the video in focus.

**Detailed description:**
Video Focus gives you a distraction-free player on regular YouTube video pages. It does not touch the YouTube home page, search, channels or Shorts.

How it works:
1. Open a video.
2. Click the Video Focus icon in the toolbar at the top of your browser. On a YouTube video page the icon turns dark and shows a small grey ON label while focus mode is on, just like the page. It is bright when the mode is off. On all other sites the icon stays normal.
3. Everything around the video disappears: recommendations, sidebar, header, title, description and comments. The page turns black and the video fills the window.
4. To leave focus mode, double-click the video. (Alt+Shift+F or the toolbar icon works too.)

Also hidden while it is on: end cards, end-screen suggestions and pause overlays.

No data collection. No network requests. No account needed. Open source (MIT).

Video Focus is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

**Single purpose:** Hide non-video page elements on youtube.com video pages to show only the video.
**Permission justification:** `storage` – saves the on/off setting. Host access to youtube.com – needed to apply the display changes on that site.
